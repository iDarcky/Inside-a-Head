# Ridely — in your own words

A money app for Uber and Bolt drivers that answers one question first: how much did I actually keep? Android, v0.22, English and Romanian.

## The spark

1. How did this start? Do you drive, or do you know drivers? Tell me about the person or moment behind it.
2. "How much did I actually keep?" When did you realise that was the one question?
3. The working name was Ride Tracker and it's Ridely "for now". How did the name come about, and what would make it final?

## Discovery

4. Who did you talk to? What do drivers do today to know their numbers (spreadsheets, notes, nothing)?
5. The PFA vs fleet split is very Romanian. How did you learn how that works, and why does it change the product so much?
6. The services that exist sell accounting. Why did you decide not to be one of them?
7. Anything a driver told you that surprised you or changed your plan?

## Definition

8. You wrote the principles before the screens: under 10 seconds to add an entry, honest numbers (known, estimated, missing), local-first, not tax advice, calm and not gamified. Which one was hardest to stick to?
9. Manual first, imports later. Why that order?
10. What's deliberately out of scope (GPS, kilometre tracking, tax filing…), and was anything painful to leave out?

## Design

11. "Rideshare cockpit": what's the idea behind that design system?
12. Material 3 Expressive components only, no hand-built ones. Why such a strict rule?
13. The home screen went through a big cut ("too many cards, too much text") down to one number. What was it like before, and how did you decide what stayed?
14. "Wrapped" for the month: where did that come from, and do drivers share it?

## Build

15. It's built with Kotlin Multiplatform so iOS can come later. Why plan for iOS now?
16. Screenshot import reads Uber and Bolt screenshots on the phone, without uploading them. How did that come about, and what was hard about it?
17. There have been 22+ versions. What's your release rhythm, and how do you test?
18. English and Romanian from the start: why?

## Launch and results

19. Who uses it now? Any real driver feedback you can share?
20. What's needed before Google Play?

## Lessons and next

21. What did building for a job you don't do yourself teach you?
22. What's next: the tax estimate for PFA drivers, imports, iOS, a final name?
