# The Retro Circuit — in your own words

A comparison engine for retro handhelds. Live at theretrocircuit.com, about 100 visitors a month, no ads.

## The spark

1. The idea started in 2022–2023. What was the moment? Were you trying to buy a handheld yourself?
2. Why retro handhelds? What's your own history with them (devices you own, games, nostalgia)?
3. Why did it take until 2025 to start building? What changed?

## Discovery

4. Who did you picture using it? A first-time buyer, an enthusiast, a collector?
5. How did people compare devices before? Which sites, Reddit threads or YouTube channels did you look at, and what was wrong with them?
6. The Retroid Pocket 4 example (one name, many SKUs with different RAM, chips and prices): when did you realise variants were the real problem?
7. Did you talk to anyone, post in communities, or test the idea before building? What did you hear?

## Definition

8. What was the MVP? What had to be in it, and what did you deliberately leave out?
9. You set a 3-month target. Was that a plan or just how it turned out? What did you cut to make it?
10. How did you decide which devices and data to start with?

## Design

11. You rejected the "gamer neon" look for a Swiss, industrial datasheet. Where did that taste come from, and was it ever a debate?
12. Press Start 2P for headings, JetBrains Mono for data, Inter for reading, and violet as the brand. How did you land on those?
13. Was there a screen or feature you redesigned several times? Why?

## Build

15. The data model (manufacturers → consoles → variants) and the admin "command center" with roles: why did those matter so much?
16. What was the hardest technical problem, and how did you get through it?
17. I saw a second repo, `the-retro-circuit-next`. What was that: a rewrite, an experiment, an abandoned path?

## Launch and results

18. How did people find it at first? Did you share it anywhere?
19. The ~100 monthly visitors with no ads: where do they come from, and what do they look at most?
20. Any feedback, messages or moments that made it feel real?
