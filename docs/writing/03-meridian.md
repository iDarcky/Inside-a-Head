# Meridian — in your own words

A calm Android calendar in the spirit of Sunrise, with events, to-dos and habits in one list. Currently v0.4.

## The spark

1. Did you use Sunrise before it shut down? What did you love about it, and what did you miss after?
2. What finally made you start Meridian? A specific frustration with Google Calendar or another app?
3. This is a native Android app in Kotlin and Compose. Your first native app? Why native and not web?

## Discovery

4. Who is it for besides you? Have you watched anyone else plan their day?
5. Which calendar, to-do and habit apps did you try, and what was wrong with each?
6. Why put events, to-dos and habits in one list? Was that the idea from day one, or did it grow?

## Definition

7. "No account, no server" and everything stays on the phone: why was that important?
8. What's in version 0.4, and what did you cut or postpone?
9. What does "calm" mean, specifically, in a calendar?

## Design

10. You studied Sunrise closely (there are reference screenshots in the repo). What did you copy on purpose, and where did you deliberately go your own way?
11. The colours: Meridian orange for actions, coral red only for today and now, neutral greys at night. How did that system come about?
12. Quick add understands sentences ("Lunch with Priya today at 1pm at Foodhallen"). How did you design that interaction?
13. Habit rings, streaks and the month heatmap: what makes a habit feature motivating rather than nagging?

## Build

14. How did building an Android app with AI compare to building for the web?
15. The hardest part so far (calendar sync, recurring events, the scrolling month, something else)?
16. Rain-aware habits move outdoor habits to a dry slot. Where did that idea come from?
17. You wrote the privacy policy before the store listing. Why so early?

## Launch and results

19. What does a Google Play release need before you'd feel ready?

## Lessons and next
