# Writing your own case studies and About page

One sheet per page. Answer in your own words, then paste the whole sheet (questions and answers) into the chat, one at a time.

**How to answer**

- Bullet points, half sentences and rambling are all fine. Write the way you'd explain it to a friend over coffee.
- Skip anything that doesn't fit. Add anything I didn't ask.
- Numbers, dates, names, little stories and "I was wrong about…" moments make a case study. Those are worth more than polish.
- Romanian is fine too if a thought comes out better that way. I'll keep your meaning and your voice.
- Don't invent results. "I don't know yet" or "nobody uses this part" is an answer.

**What I'll do with it**

Shape it into the page: put it in order, cut repetition, fix typos, keep your wording and tone. You'll see the result on a preview before anything goes live, and you can still edit every word in Keystatic afterwards.

**Sheets**

1. `00-about.md`: the About page
2. `01-the-retro-circuit.md`
3. `02-setlists-md.md`
4. `03-meridian.md`
5. `04-ridely.md`

Each project sheet follows the same arc (spark, discovery, definition, design, build, launch, lessons), so the four case studies read as a set, but the questions are specific to each project.
