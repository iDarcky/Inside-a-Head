# About page — in your own words

The manifesto ("Ideas are messy.") stays at the top. These questions are for everything under it.

## You

1. How would you introduce yourself to someone at a party who asked "so what do you do?" Two or three sentences.
2. What were you like as a kid? Anything that already pointed at "always making plans, coming up with stories and ideas"?
3. Why "inside a head"? You told me once it's because you always lived inside your head. Tell it again, longer, the way you'd tell it.
4. You called yourself "idealist builder, visionary PM, musician". Is that still right? What would you change?
5. One thing people are usually surprised to learn about you.

## The product manager part

6. How did you end up in product management? Was it planned, or did you fall into it?
7. Without naming the company: what kind of product did you work on, and who used it? What was the hardest part of the job?
8. A moment from those four years you're proud of. Something you shipped, fixed, or a call you got right.
9. A moment you got wrong, and what it taught you.
10. What do you think a good PM actually does that a bad one doesn't?

## The builder part

11. When did you start building your own things, and what pushed you to start?
12. You build with AI as your engineering partner. How does that actually work day to day? What do you do, and what does the AI do?
13. What's harder than people think about building this way? What's easier?
14. Which tools do you reach for every day (apps, editors, AI tools, notebooks, anything)?
15. What are you learning right now?

## The rest of you

16. Music: what do you play, where, and how does it feed into the rest of what you do?
17. "A messy coffee shop with a huge library": is that still the picture of this studio? Describe the room.
18. What do you hope someone feels or does after visiting this site?
19. Where do you want to be in two years? The north star you mentioned was an agency helping small creators and builders. Still true?
20. Anything else you want on this page that I haven't asked about?
